# Wetland (aggregated) cover (`ceh_landcover_wetland`)

In-cell share of Sum of Bog and Fen, Marsh and Swamp LCM classes on the model grid (percent × 100).

## Source and licence

## Pipeline

`ceh` rebuild step → merge as `ceh_landcover_wetland`.

## Role

**Data** — land-cover predictor documentation.
