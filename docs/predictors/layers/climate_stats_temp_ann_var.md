# Annual temperature variability (`climate_stats_temp_ann_var`)

Standard deviation of twelve monthly WorldClim average-temperature bands (°C).

## Source and licence

WorldClim 2.1 — academic / non-commercial. See [worldclim_2_1](../sources/worldclim_2_1.md).

## Pipeline

`climate` → `calculate_climate_statistics` → `climate_stats_temp_ann_var`.

## Role

**Data** — climate predictor documentation.
