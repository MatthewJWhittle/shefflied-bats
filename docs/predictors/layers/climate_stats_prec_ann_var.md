# Annual precipitation variability (`climate_stats_prec_ann_var`)

Standard deviation of twelve monthly WorldClim precipitation bands (mm).

## Source and licence

WorldClim 2.1 — academic / non-commercial; rasters not in git.

## Pipeline

`climate` → `calculate_climate_statistics` → `climate_stats_prec_ann_var`.

## Role
