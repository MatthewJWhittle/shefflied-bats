# Annual mean wind speed (`climate_stats_wind_ann_avg`)

Mean of twelve monthly WorldClim wind-speed bands.

## Source and licence

WorldClim 2.1 — academic / non-commercial. See [worldclim_2_1](../sources/worldclim_2_1.md).

## Pipeline

`climate` → `calculate_climate_statistics` → `climate_stats_wind_ann_avg`.

## Role

**Data** — climate predictor documentation.
