# Upland heathland (aggregated) cover (`ceh_landcover_upland_heathland`)

In-cell share of Sum of Heather and shrub and Heather grassland on the model grid (percent × 100).

## Source and licence

UKCEH Land Cover Map 2023. Licence and access: [ukceh_lcm_2023](../sources/ukceh_lcm_2023.md).

## Pipeline

`ceh` rebuild step → merge as `ceh_landcover_upland_heathland`.

## Role

**Data** — land-cover predictor documentation.
