# EA LiDAR Composite DTM (`ea_lidar_dtm`)

Bare-earth elevation product behind `terrain_dtm` and terrain derivatives.

## Provider and product

**Environment Agency** — LiDAR Composite Digital Terrain Model (DTM) 1 m.

## Notes

Multi-year composite, not a single flight date. Coverage gaps become missing
cells after warp. Vertical accuracy of contributing surveys is about ±15 cm
RMSE (EA metadata).
