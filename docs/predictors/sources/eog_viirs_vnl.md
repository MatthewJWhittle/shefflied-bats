# EOG VIIRS night lights (`eog_viirs_vnl`)

Annual night-lights radiance behind `alan_viirs_radiance`.

## Provider and product

**Earth Observation Group (Payne Institute) / VIIRS** — EOG annual VNL v2.2
`average_masked` composite. Project canonical year is **2025**.

## Licence and access

CC-BY-4.0. Products: [EOG VNL](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/).

## Citation

Earth Observation Group, Payne Institute for Public Policy.

## Notes

Native information is roughly 500 m; the model band is bilinear-warped to
100 m cells. Temporal mismatch with older occurrence years is expected. See
also [alan-layer.md](../alan-layer.md).
