# UKCEH Land Cover Map 2023 (`ukceh_lcm_2023`)

10 m national land-cover classification used for in-cell and neighbourhood
`ceh_landcover_*` predictors.

## Provider and product

**UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology** — Land Cover Map 2023 10 m raster.

## Licence and access

UKCEH EIDC standard click-through licence (non-commercial). Product page:
[UKCEH Land Cover Maps](https://www.ceh.ac.uk/data/ukceh-land-cover-maps).
Licence text:
[standard click-through](https://eidc.ceh.ac.uk/licences/standard-click-through/plain).

## Citation

UKCEH Land Cover Map 2023.

## Notes

Class codes and legend follow the published LCM 2023 product. Layer docs name
the specific class each band aggregates.
