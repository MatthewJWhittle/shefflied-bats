# WorldClim 2.1 (`worldclim_2_1`)

Global climate normals used for `climate_bioclim_*` and `climate_stats_*`
predictors.

## Provider and product

**WorldClim** — version 2.1 bioclimatic variables and monthly climate grids
(~30 arc-second native resolution).

## Licence and access

Academic / non-commercial terms; redistribution or commercial use needs prior
permission. See [WorldClim about](https://worldclim.org/about.html). Download
tiles:
[UCDavis geodata](https://geodata.ucdavis.edu/climate/worldclim/2_1/tiles/iso/).

## Citation

Fick, S.E. and R.J. Hijmans, 2017. WorldClim 2: new 1-km spatial resolution
climate surfaces for global land areas. *International Journal of Climatology*
37 (12): 4302–4315.

## Notes

Normals are approximately 1970–2000 and are not matched to bat-record years.
Layers upsample to the 100 m model grid; fine cell-to-cell climate structure is
not native.
